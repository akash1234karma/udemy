# Electric Vehicle Comparison for Sales Fleet

## Tesla Model Y
- Starting at an MSRP of $37,795
- EPA-estimated range of around 260 miles
- Key features include a 10.2" cluster display and 15.5" center display

## Ford Mustang Mach-E
- Key Types: The Tesla Model Y supports different types of keys, including phone keys, key cards, and key fobs. Key fobs require a CR2032 3V battery and are compatible with all Model 3 and Model Y vehicles. The retail price for a Model 3/Y Key Fob is $175.
- Key Limit: Model Y supports up to 19 keys at a time, with four of those keys being key fobs. When the limit of keys is reached, a key must be deleted before adding a new one.
- Key Accessories: Tesla vehicles, including Model Y, come with two Key Cards and one bifold wallet for easy entry, operation, and transport. Additional backup key cards and key fobs can be purchased from the Tesla Shop.

## Recommendation
Both the Tesla Model Y and Ford Mustang Mach-E are strong contenders for our sales fleet. The Tesla Model Y offers a competitive starting price and a decent range, while the Ford Mustang Mach-E provides unique key features and accessories. Depending on the specific needs and preferences of the fleet, either of these electric vehicles would be a great choice.
